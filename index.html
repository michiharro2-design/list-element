Angela's Cinnamon Roll Recipe for best

cineman roll

Indgredients

For the dough:

  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 2¼ teaspoon yeast
  • 1¼ cup granulated Ssugar
  • 1 egg plus 1 egg yolk
  • 1¼ cup butter
  • 3 cup butter flour

    • For the filling :

  • 2⅓ cup dark brown sugar
  • 1 1½ table spoons grounf cinnamon
  • 1¼ cup butter

    • Instruction

    1. mix the milk with the yeast ,sugar , eggs
    2. melt the butter and add to the mixture
    3. add in the flour and mix untill combined into a dough
    4.knead the dough for 10 minutes
    5 transfer the dough into a large bowl and cover with the plastic wrap. leave it somewhere to rise for 2 hours.
    6.ahter the dough has double in size roll it out into a large rectangle
    7.Melt the butter for the filling and mix in the sugar and cinnamon. Spread the filling onto the dough then roll the dough into a swiss roll
    8. Cut the roll into 3cm sections and place flat into a baking tray. Pre-heat the oven to 350F or 180C, then bake the rolls for 20-25min until lightly brown.
